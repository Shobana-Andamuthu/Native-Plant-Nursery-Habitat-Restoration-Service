Why Native Plant Ecosystems Matter

Native plants have co-evolved alongside our regional birds, insects, and soil microbes for millennia. When we plant natives, we rebuild the foundational food web upon which all wildlife depends.

Local Eco-type Adaptation We source seeds strictly within verified regional eco-regions to preserve natural genetic fitness and phenology.
Deep Root Hydrology & Soil Building Native roots penetrate up to 15 feet into subsoil, sequestering carbon and opening channels that absorb heavy rainfall.
Zero Pesticide Propagation Our entire nursery operation is strictly free from synthetic neonicotinoids, guaranteeing safe forage for delicate pollinator species.
